Izu Peninsula

The Izu Peninsula is a mountainous peninsula south-west of Tokyo with beaches on the coast and hot-spring towns inland. Our first homes are in Shimoda and Shuzenji.

Where is the Izu Peninsula?

The Izu Peninsula is in Shizuoka Prefecture, south-west of Tokyo. Atami, at its northern end, is about 80 minutes from Tokyo by limited express, and Shimoda, at the southern tip, is about 2 hours 35 minutes by direct train (japan-guide.com; Ekitan). Full routes and fares are in Izu Peninsula from Tokyo: trains, fares and why to stay a week.

Which towns are on the Izu Peninsula?

  • Shimoda. Port town at the southern tip. Beaches, a fish market and Japan’s largest kinmedai landings.
  • Shuzenji. Inland hot-spring town, 35 minutes by local line from Mishima (japan-guide.com).
  • Atami and Ito. The closest towns to Tokyo, with the most hotels.

When is the Izu Peninsula at its best?

The official swimming season at Izu beaches runs from around mid-July to late August (japan-guide.com). Outside summer the towns are much quieter, and many restaurants and shops close early (The Digital Nomad Asia). That quiet is the reason to come for a week instead of a weekend.

## Noki (English)

> Noki is RIVIA&CO.’s English-language guide for people who want to stay a month in small-town Japan, work remotely or test a town before buying an akiya (vacant house).

- [Noki](https://rivia-co.com/noki): Homes for a week, a month or a season in small-town Japan, and plain guides to akiya, remote work and life outside the big cities from the people who run them.
- [Izu Peninsula](https://rivia-co.com/noki/izu-peninsula.html): The Izu Peninsula is a mountainous peninsula south-west of Tokyo with beaches on the coast and onsen towns inland. Where to base yourself and how to get there.
- [Kinmedai: Japan’s golden-eye snapper and where to eat it](https://rivia-co.com/noki/guides/kinmedai.html): Kinmedai is a red, golden-eyed deep-sea fish eaten simmered, seared or raw. What it is, when to eat it and why Shimoda is the port to try it in.
- [Buying an akiya in Japan? Live in one for a month first](https://rivia-co.com/noki/guides/akiya-try-before-you-buy.html): Japan has about nine million empty homes, and foreigners can buy them. Before you do, spend a month in the town. Here’s what it shows you that a listing can’t.
- [Izu Peninsula from Tokyo: trains, fares and why to stay a week](https://rivia-co.com/noki/guides/izu-peninsula-from-tokyo.html): How to get from Tokyo to the Izu Peninsula by train, with times and fares to Atami, Ito, Shimoda and Shuzenji, the best season and why a house beats a day trip.

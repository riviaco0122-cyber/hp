About

About Okaeri Japan

Okaeri Japan is written by the team that runs the homes. This page says who we are, how the guides are put together and how to tell us we got something wrong.

Who runs Okaeri Japan?

Okaeri Japan is run by RIVIA&CO. LLC, a company based in Tokyo that renovates older houses in regional Japan and runs them as places to stay. Our first home, NODE Shimoda, is in Shimoda on the Izu Peninsula. We are preparing homes in Shuzenji and Yaizu.

What does “okaeri” mean?

Okaeri (おかえり) is what people in Japan say when someone comes home. It means “welcome back”. We chose it because the guests we like best are the ones who return.

How are the guides written?

  • Every figure and rule links to its source. Where we can, that is a government office or the operator itself, with the date we checked.
  • We say which parts are our own experience. Anything about our homes comes from running them.
  • We change the date only when the content changes. Fares, opening hours and visa rules move, so check the linked source before you book.
  • We do not sell houses or give legal advice. Guides about akiya and visas are general information.

How do you tell us something is wrong?

Write to us and we will check the source and correct the guide.

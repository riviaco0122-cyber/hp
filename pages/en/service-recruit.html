Overview

Across industries, we solve staff shortages at regional companies end to end — from hiring plans to outreach and retention.

Service areaNationwide · online

Issues & Solutions

Do any of these sound familiar?

"No applicants" and "people quit right away" are problems of business planning, organization and communication. We redesign hiring from the management level.

ISSUE 01Business challenge

Staff shortages cap your sales and growth

What's happening on the ground

"We don't have enough people, so we limit the work we take on and our opening hours. We can't start new businesses either."

What RIVIA does
  1. 1

    Working back from your business plan, we design a staffing plan: which roles, how many people, and by when.

  2. 2

    We treat hiring not as filling vacancies but as an investment in growth, and start with the highest-priority roles.

Business metrics improved
  • Staffing rate
  • Volume of work you can accept
  • Opportunity loss from staff shortages

ISSUE 02Business challenge

Hiring costs are sunk costs, not managed as investments

What's happening on the ground

"We paid over JPY 500,000 to a job site, got one applicant, and they didn't show up. Nothing remains each time."

What RIVIA does
  1. 1

    Centered on a careers website, we optimally combine job search engines, scouting, ads and social media.

  2. 2

    We measure applications, hires and costs by channel and concentrate budget on what works.

Business metrics improved
  • Cost per hire
  • Hires by channel
  • Media costs

ISSUE 03Business challenge

No reason to be chosen, so you lose on salary and benefits

What's happening on the ground

"We've given up because we're rural and unknown. We can't beat big companies on pay or days off."

What RIVIA does
  1. 1

    Through interviews with management and staff, we put into words what makes working with you special.

  2. 2

    Through a careers site and employee voices, we deliver that appeal to people who value working in the regions.

Business metrics improved
  • Applications
  • Share of target applicants
  • Offer acceptance rate

ISSUE 04Business challenge

New hires don't stay, so the investment isn't recouped

What's happening on the ground

"New hires leave within months saying the job wasn't what they expected, wasting money and time."

What RIVIA does
  1. 1

    We convey the real job before hiring and design selection criteria and casual interviews to prevent mismatches.

  2. 2

    We set up onboarding support so new hires stay and thrive.

Business metrics improved
  • Early turnover rate
  • Retention rate
  • Recouping hiring & training costs

FAQ

FAQ

QHow is this different from posting on a job board?
A

Instead of paying listing fees indefinitely, we build up a careers site and content as your own assets, creating a system that brings direct applications from multiple channels. Job boards are used as one part of the mix when needed.

QCan we ask for just social media management or interview coaching?
A

You can consult us about individual initiatives. But results depend on the link to your business plan and hiring targets, so we first clarify your current challenges and then propose the best approach.

QCan businesses outside tourism use this service?
A

Yes. We support hiring at regional companies in any industry, including manufacturing, construction, care, retail and tourism.

QCan a regional company attract people from the cities?
A

Yes. We can effectively reach urban talent who value U-turn or I-turn moves, contributing to a region, or flexible ways of working.

QDo you support retention after hiring?
A

Yes. From selection design that prevents mismatches to onboarding support for new hires, we support people staying and thriving.

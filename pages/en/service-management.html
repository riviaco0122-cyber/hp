FAQ

FAQ

QCan I open an inn with no hospitality experience at all?
A

Yes. We guide you from site selection and permits through launch preparation and teach you the practical side of operations. You can also leave operations to RIVIA.

QCan I use this service for a vacation rental or small inn that is already operating?
A

This service is for people starting a vacation rental or small inn. For marketing an existing property, please consult us about Web Marketing Support.

QCan a company consult you about starting a vacation rental as a new business?
A

Yes. We also work with builders, real estate companies and other businesses that want to start accommodation as a new venture, supporting them from business planning to launch and operation.

QHow is pricing structured?
A

If you entrust operations to us, we charge 10–20% of revenue depending on the scope of work (initial costs such as acquiring and renovating the property are the owner's responsibility). There is also a plan where you co-invest the initial costs with RIVIA and share profits according to the investment ratio.

QWhat do the monthly reports tell me?
A

Each month we send a summary of occupancy, average rate, revenue and reviews, and hold an online review session. We decide together what to do the following month.

QCan you handle nighttime emergencies?
A

Yes. With a multilingual AI chatbot and 24-hour online support, we handle late-night inquiries and on-site emergencies on your behalf.

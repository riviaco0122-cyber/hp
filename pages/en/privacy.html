Privacy Policy

RIVIA&CO. LLC (the "Company") considers the proper handling of customers' personal information to be a social responsibility. We comply with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other applicable laws, and handle personal information as follows.

1. Collection of personal information

Through our contact form and job application form, the Company collects, by fair means, your name, company or property name, phone number, email address, inquiry details, CV, résumé and other information you enter or send.

2. Purpose of use

We use the personal information we collect within the scope of the following purposes.
(1) Responding to and contacting you about inquiries and consultations
(2) Proposing our services, providing quotes, and concluding and performing contracts
(3) Recruitment screening and communications about hiring
(4) Work incidental to the above

3. Provision to third parties

Except as required by law, we do not provide personal information to third parties without your consent.

4. Outsourcing

To the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use, we may entrust the handling of personal information to external providers such as form-submission services. In such cases, we exercise necessary and appropriate supervision over them.

5. Analytics tools

To understand how our website is used and to improve it, we use Google Analytics, an analytics tool provided by Google LLC. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect traffic data in a form that does not identify individuals. You can refuse this by disabling cookies in your browser settings. For details, please see the Google Analytics Terms of Service and Google's policies and terms .

6. Security measures

We take necessary and appropriate measures to prevent leakage, loss or damage of personal information and otherwise keep it secure.

7. Disclosure, correction and suspension of use

If you request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion or suspension of use of your personal information, we will respond without delay in accordance with the law after confirming your identity.

8. Contact

For inquiries about the handling of personal information, please contact us via our contact form.

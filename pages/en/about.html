About Us

About Us

Mission

Turning the hidden value of our regions
into Japan's vitality.

Vacant houses and idle real estate,

food and nature, culture and the ways people live.

Our regions still hold

a wealth of untapped value.

For the land itself,

and for the people who live there,

what is truly needed?

We stand on the ground ourselves

and engage deeply with each region.

We uncover dormant value

and turn it into community building and tourism.

One by one, we grow the number of places

where people visit, work

and can keep on living.

When our regions thrive,

Japan thrives.

That is what we believe.

Vision

Stays where you travel as if you live there,
for towns all across Japan.

What we will work on over the medium term to realize our mission.

  1. 01Discover

    Discover

    We unearth the resources sleeping in each region — vacant houses and idle real estate, food, nature, culture and the ways people live.

  2. 02Refine

    Refine

    With design and technology, we revive and create inns and tourism experiences where guests can live the everyday life of the place.

  3. 03Deliver

    Deliver

    Through web marketing, we make sure that value reaches travelers in Japan and abroad who are looking for it.

  4. 04Circulate

    Circulate

    We bring visitors and jobs to the region, leading to a sustainable economic cycle and community building.

  • For travelersNew value in staying — blending into the everyday life of the place.
  • For the regionAn economic cycle where dormant resources earn, creating jobs and connections.
  • For ownersA future where burdensome assets and facilities become businesses that generate income.

Values

What we value

  • 03

    We don't stop at good intentions — we answer with numbers and results.

  • 04

    We don't settle for one-off efforts; we choose approaches that stay and circulate in the region.

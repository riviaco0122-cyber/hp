Issues & Solutions

Do any of these sound familiar?

Marketing problems aren't about channels. They come from a business structure where you can't control your own customer acquisition.

Relying on outside sites, you've lost control of profit and pricing

What's happening on the ground

  1. 1

ISSUE 02Business challenge

Sales targets and marketing aren't connected

What RIVIA does

  2. 2

    We define each channel's role and budget, and turn it into a prioritized action plan.

Business metrics improved
  • Sales by channel

  • Inquiries & bookings

FAQ

FAQ

QCan businesses outside the accommodation industry use this service?
A

Yes. Restaurants, retailers, tourism businesses, regional SMEs — businesses in any industry are welcome.

QIs it just social media management?
A

No. Without limiting ourselves to any one channel, we propose the right mix for your business — website development, SEO and owned media, Google Business Profile, web ads, social media and repeat-customer initiatives.

QOur website is old, or we don't have one. Is that okay?
A

Yes. We can build a new website or renew your current one so that it drives inquiries and bookings. If your existing site can be used, we can start with improvement proposals.

QWe don't have a single high-quality photo or video. Is that okay?
A

Don't worry. Our professional photographer will visit on site and shoot and edit the images that will stand out most on your website, web ads and social media.

QHow much ad budget do we need each month?
A

You can start small, from a few tens of thousands of yen a month. Rather than committing to one channel, we recommend gradually shifting budget to the channels that perform while watching CPA.

Concept

Experience the charm of Japan
as if you lived there.

We don't simply provide places to stay.
With design and technology, we transform the dormant resources of a region — vacant houses and idle real estate — into "places where you can experience everyday local life."
New value for travelers, and a sustainable economic cycle for the region.

  • 01Stay

    Turning a vacant house into an inn

    RIVIA revives long-empty houses as inns, so guests can stay as part of local life.

  • 02Walk

    From the inn, out into the town

    Walk the lantern-lit lanes, eat and shop at local stores. Travelers' spending spreads beyond the inn into the town.

  • 03Connect

    Connecting with the land, and coming back

    Food, climate, attractions and people. Connecting with a place's charm brings guests back — and creates income and jobs for the region.

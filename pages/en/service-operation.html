ISSUE 02Challenge

A house that can't be sold or torn down

What we often hear

"Demolition costs millions of yen, and it's too old to sell. I can neither dispose of it nor use it."

What RIVIA does
  1. 1

    Based on on-site and market research, we compare the finances of demolishing, selling and using the house and present them to you.

  2. 2

    RIVIA handles everything from renovation to operation. If you'd rather let it go, we can also buy it.

What changes
  • Demolition cost
  • Income from the house
  • Value of the house

ISSUE 03Challenge

FAQ

FAQ

QCan you look at a vacant house even if it's run-down and leaking?
A

Yes. As long as the structure (columns and foundation) is sound, it can be revived through renovation. We'll start with an on-site inspection.

QI don't want to let it go. Can you lease it while I keep ownership?
A

Yes. We can propose both a plan where we sign a lease and pay you monthly rent, and a plan where you sell.

QA real estate agent told me demolition would cost several million yen.
A

If you use it as accommodation instead of demolishing it, there is a good chance you can avoid demolition costs entirely and turn it into an income-generating asset.

QWhat areas and properties do you cover?
A

Vacant houses and idle real estate anywhere in Japan — detached houses, traditional houses (kominka), apartments and more. Start by telling us the location and condition of the property.

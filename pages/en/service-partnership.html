What's happening on the ground

"We don't know whether it can be used as accommodation or how much renovation it needs, so we can't commit to preparing it for sale."

  1. 1

FAQ

FAQ

QWhat kinds of properties qualify?
A

Detached houses, traditional houses, small apartment buildings and other properties that could be operated as accommodation. Even for rural or older properties, please consult us first.

QWho sells to the investors?
A

The real estate company introduces and sells the properties to investors through seminars, newsletters and so on. RIVIA selects the properties, calculates yields and prepares sales materials, and presents at seminars on request.

QIs the yield to investors guaranteed?
A

It depends on the contract. Besides paying a fixed rent, we can return profits linked to revenue. We propose terms suited to the property and the investor's wishes.

QHow does RIVIA earn its income?
A

After purchase, RIVIA launches and operates the property and receives a portion of accommodation revenue as payment for operations. Investors receive returns in the form of rent or profit distributions.
